  - type: prose
    id: what
    title: What adopting RISA means
    body: |
      - **The toolbox is open.** The brief, the SOP, its annexes and the XLSForm template are free to use and adapt. [Open the toolbox](/toolbox).
      - **A host authority provides** a focal point from the start, support with site access and drone flight authorisations, a data-sharing agreement, and the organisation that takes over, named early.
      - **The partners provide** in-country mentoring where possible, remote support, webinars and short videos, on a best-effort basis. There is no ticketed helpdesk and no promised response time.
