  - type: prose
    id: what
    title: What RISA is
    body: |
      RISA is a rapid way to map what exists on the ground in a displacement site, and whether it works. Field teams record each water point, latrine, health post, distribution point and blocked road, with its status and location. Drone or satellite imagery shows the site's layout. The two are combined into one standard map that everyone in the response can use.

      It is open, low-tech and independent of any software, so national teams can run it themselves. Nothing is patented: use it and adapt it.

  - type: prose
    id: mira
    title: RISA and MIRA
    body: |
      RISA does not replace MIRA or other needs assessments, and it does not interview people. Sector assessments capture needs, but rarely show how a site is laid out or which services work. RISA adds the physical picture: what is there, and what is working. Community needs assessment stays with IOM and the clusters.

  - type: prose
    id: handover
    title: Built for handover
    short: Handover
    body: |
      RISA fills the gap in the first weeks, until a cluster or sector lead (such as IOM or CCCM) or the national authority takes over, usually around week 3. The data and tools stay with the national authority, in open formats, as agreed in the data-sharing agreement.

      What we ask of a host authority:

      - a focal point who works with the team from the start and takes over the maps and data;
      - agreement on priority sites, and support with site access and drone flight authorisations;
      - a data-sharing agreement: who owns the data, who may see it, where it is stored and for how long;
      - the organisation that will take over at handover, named early;
      - help with local logistics where possible {tbc: to agree per deployment}.

  - type: governance
    id: governance
    title: Governance
    body: |
      Governance is deliberately light: a letter of intent, then an MOU or terms of reference, with no binding teaming agreement until there is funding for a specific project. Any partner can opt out of side initiatives without affecting RISA. The working-together terms are being drafted {tbc: draft due 30 Nov 2026}. The partners meet online every month and in person about every four months.
