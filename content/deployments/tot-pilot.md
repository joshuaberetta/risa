## Why

Before training national authorities, the partners need a cohort of trainers who have all run RISA the same way.

## What's planned

A pilot course for partner rosters (Kobo, MapAction, Atlas Logistique), with a SIMEX on realistic scenario data. Atlas covers direct training costs; partners cover travel and staff time. Two further courses for national authorities, Flying Labs operators and national NGOs follow in year two.

The Lyon workshop added regional training of trainers through the CONNECT proposal, and a three-hour webinar with one hour per partner {tbc}.
