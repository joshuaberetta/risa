## Why

HNPW is where UNDAC, OCHA and donors meet. It is a chance to seek recognition for RISA in learning pathways, to make the case for aerial and drone assessment in UNDAC, and to engage donors.

## What's planned

- A booth and side sessions.
- The next in-person RISA workshop.
- A presentation at the UNDAC advisory board day, which reaches the network's member states and donors such as Norway.
