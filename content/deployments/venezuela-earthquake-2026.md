## The situation

A magnitude 7.5 earthquake struck near Caracas.

## What we did

Atlas Logistique flew the sites by drone. Kobo supported remotely with adapted assessment forms, KoboToolbox accounts and Spanish-language guidance. Who produced the maps is to be confirmed {tbc}.

## What it showed

The drone orthomosaic revealed handwashing stations, latrines, child-friendly spaces and health services that satellite imagery could not reliably show. This is the clearest case so far for the aerial input.
