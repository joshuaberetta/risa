## The setting

RISA in Brief v3 and SOP v3 list Mozambique as the SADC simulation exercise (SIMEX). The earlier project records describe severe flooding in early 2026 that displaced large numbers of people {tbc: exercise or response to be aligned}.

## What we did

Working alongside UNDAC, OCHA and MapAction, the partners ran RISA end to end for the first time. Kobo built the observation form. Atlas Logistique and Kobo collected field data with KoboCollect, and Atlas Logistique made the drone orthomosaics. MapAction produced the maps. Assessment coordination is to be confirmed {tbc}. The output was the static three-page product.

## What it showed

The core workflow works: three inputs collected in parallel, fused into one product, and shared within days. The static PDF, though, loses much of the richness of the combined drone, field and imagery data. That is why RISA now pairs the printed map with an online view.

The field and GIS teams worked in the same place, so large datasets moved on external hard drives. With remote GIS teams, that will not work.
