## Why

Before RISA can be handed over, the partners need one agreed version of the method, the toolbox and the products.

## What we decided

- **A joint initiative, not a new organisation.** Three core partners with an equal share, and UNDAC/OCHA as endorsers (clearance pending).
- **Light governance.** A letter of intent, then an MOU or terms of reference. No binding agreement until there is funding for a specific project.
- **The pilot scope.** Displacement camps in the first days of a sudden-onset response, until a cluster or sector lead takes over around week 3. Community needs assessment stays with IOM and the clusters.
- **A two-layer toolbox.** A two-page brief for heads of NDMAs, and one software-neutral SOP with annexes for technicians. The printable drone checklist stays separate. Link to existing tutorials rather than write software guides.
- **Open standards.** XLSForm, standard field names, symbology and map elements. Open source and low-tech first; ArcGIS is not required.
- **A printed map and an online view.** The printed map carries a QR code to the online view, which is the source of truth, filterable by sector and offline-first.
- **Handover.** The SOP and annexes, in-country mentoring before leaving, remote support, webinars and short videos.
- **No ticketed helpdesk.** A contact form, answered on a best-effort basis.
- **The logo.** Option 10, with tweaks.
- **Rhythm.** Monthly online check-ins, and an in-person workshop about every four months.
