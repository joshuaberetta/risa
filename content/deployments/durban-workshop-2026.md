## Why

Southern Africa is where RISA was first run, in the SADC SIMEX. A regional workshop reaches several national authorities at once.

## What's planned

MapAction invited the RISA partners to a session with SHOC and SADC member states (Madagascar, Malawi, Mozambique, Zambia and Zimbabwe). The partners will present the pilot toolbox: RISA in Brief, the SOP and its annexes, and the XLSForm template. They will listen to what national teams need to run it themselves.

The pitch is operational value. Technical webinars follow in January.
