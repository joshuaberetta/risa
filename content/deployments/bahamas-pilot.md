## Why

A capacity-strengthening project with a national disaster management authority is a chance to test the SOP and RISA training in a hurricane-prone small island state.

## What's planned

MapAction and Kobo run a project building information-management skills in the authority: 3–5 staff, in monthly sessions. It is the pilot site to validate the SOP. How Atlas Logistique is included is still being explored {tbc}.
