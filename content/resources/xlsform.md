details:
  - "Each submission is one object: pick the sector, then the feature type, then give its feature ID, status, count, location and up to three photos."
  - "Sectors: WASH, shelter, health, protection, food, logistics, access, energy, environment, commerce and other."
  - "Status choices match the map colours: Working, Partly working, Not working, Can't see. Partly working and Not working ask why."
  - Site details carry over from the last submission, so collectors fill them once per site.
  - Plain XLSForm. It runs on KoboToolbox, ODK Central, Ona or any other XLSForm platform, offline.
  - The field names are the contract the RISA dashboard reads. Never rename or delete a question or a code once data exists; add only, and change the version.
  - "The package: the template (with codebook and country checklist sheets), the codebook, and the mapping from earlier forms."
  - English base form. Countries add their own languages, translated with AI and reviewed by a native speaker.
