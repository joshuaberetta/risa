details:
  - "Steps: mission preparation, field data collection, data processing, GIS integration, dissemination, updating and handover."
  - "Principles: rapid, accurate, standardised, operational, scalable and collaborative."
  - "Pilot scope: displacement camps and collective centres in the first days of a sudden-onset response, until a cluster or sector lead takes over around week 3."
  - "Standards: XLSForm, open source and low-tech first. ArcGIS is not required."
  - The main text is non-technical. Field-level detail is in Annexes A to F, each owned by a partner.
  - English is the reference. Translations are made with AI, then reviewed by a native speaker.
  - Replaces working draft v2 (file v2.1).
