72 hours

Rapid Infrastructure and Service Assessment

The first 72 hours decide the response.

They’re also when we know least. RISA turns drone imagery and direct field observation into one operational map of a displacement site, within days of the site being set up, and answers three questions.

  1. 01Can we reach people? Access routes, by status.
  2. 02Which services still work? Water, sanitation, health, protection.
  3. 03Where do we act first? Priority gaps, ranked.

Day 1–2collectThree inputs in parallel

Three inputs, one common operational picture.

RISA records what physically exists on the ground. It complements, rather than replaces, MIRA and rapid needs assessments.

Aerial imagery

A drone orthomosaic of the site, flown at 120 m for about 4 cm per pixel, or recent satellite imagery where flying isn’t possible.

HI/Atlas Logistique · UAV operations

Field observation

A standard KoboCollect survey, recorded by direct observation rather than interviews, that works offline and syncs when there’s signal.

Kobo · field data

GIS integration

Both brought together on one map with common symbology, a georeferenced inventory of every water point, latrine and service.

MapAction · GIS

  4. Day 3–44

  5. Day 3–55

    Dissemination
