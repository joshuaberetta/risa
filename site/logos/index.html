Logo concepts

Eight marks for a method.

Each concept says something about how RISA works: looking straight down, combining inputs, checking the ground. All of them work in one colour, survive a photocopier and read at 16 px, and none uses the red, amber or green reserved for status on the map. Each also has to step back when a national authority’s logo leads.

Concepts for discussion. Final artwork would be redrawn and optically adjusted once a direction is chosen.
